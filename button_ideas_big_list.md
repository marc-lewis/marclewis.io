# Button ideas: the big list

The curated shortlist lives in `button_ideas.md` (ideas 1-15). This is the rest of the brainstorm: 400 more, numbered on from 16, grouped by theme. Effort is deliberately untagged here - some are a line of CSS, some are weekends, some are jokes about weekends. The only rule applied: each one is a distinct image, not a rewording of its neighbour.

Notes:
- Anything mentioning sound can be done today with WebAudio or SpeechSynthesis - no audio files needed
- Anything mentioning localStorage is serverless and per-browser
- Nothing in this list strobes or flashes; the site promises (see idea 384)
